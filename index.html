RSVP

Let us know if you can make it, and then help us with the surprise. We’re gathering memories, photographs and songs to bind into a book for her. Please send those even if you can’t be there. The book is the part of this that shouldn’t miss anyone, and distance is no reason to be left out of it.

And none of it has to arrive at once. Send your answer now, then come back to this page whenever you like to add a memory, photographs or a song. You can send as many times as you like, and we will put it all together at our end.
