Jeannie turns 70

Write her a letter

We’re binding letters, photographs and songs into a book for her seventieth. It goes to print on Sunday, November 29.

Please send one even if you can’t come. Especially if you can’t come. A letter from someone who couldn’t be in the room is the part she will read twice.

Three ways. Whichever suits you — do as many as you like, they all end up in the same book.
