Saturday, January 23

Jeannie turns 70

Write her a letter

We’re binding letters, photographs and songs into a book for her seventieth. It goes to print on Sunday, November 29.

Please send one even if you can’t come. Especially if you can’t come. A letter from someone who couldn’t be in the room is the part she will read twice.

Three ways. Whichever suits you — they all end up in the same book.

  • Handwrite it. Photograph the note and upload it. The book will have your own handwriting in it, which beats anything typed.
  • Type it. Straight into the box on the next page, or in Word or Pages at your own pace and upload the file.
  • Speak it. Record a voice message on the next page and just talk. We’ll listen and write it down, and she gets to hear your voice.

And a favourite photograph of you, however old. She knows what she looks like; it’s you she wants in there.

Send your letter

Five minutes, and nothing to sign up for.
Come back as many times as you like before the 29th.
